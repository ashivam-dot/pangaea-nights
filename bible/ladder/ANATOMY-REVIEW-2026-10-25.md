# Cambrian visual repair

Direct review of run 40122729549's eight base and seven alternate option stills found substantial errors:
both Opabinia takes resemble walking crabs; Hallucigenia is an upright tower; both Anomalocaris takes have
fish-like bodies and fins. The preview is held and its render was canceled. CLIP relevance and exposure scores
did not establish anatomical accuracy. No full video or audio approval is claimed.

Keep the spoken script, narrator, ranking, fact and format. Change the affected image directions to show the
whole body and its distinguishing structures. The release reviewer now receives the same anatomy requirements
as the painter. A replacement still needs original gates and full rendered audiovisual review.
